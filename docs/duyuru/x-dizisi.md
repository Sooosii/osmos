# X (Twitter) dizisi

✍️ **Taslak** — biçim işi olduğu için yazılmış hâlde. Yine de yüksek sesle
oku; ağzından çıkmayacak bir cümle varsa değiştir.

**Zaman:** Show HN gönderisiyle aynı gün. Dizinin sonunda HN bağlantısını
alıntıla ("tartışma burada") — iki kanal birbirini besler.

**Görsel kuralı:** her gönderide görsel olsun. X'te görselsiz gönderi
neredeyse görünmez. En iyisi **ekran kaydı videosu** (TikTok senaryosundaki
ilk 10 saniye burada da çalışır); yoksa `docs/preview/` altındaki üç kare.

---

**1/6** — kanca + video (uzayda süzülme)

> Perfume writing is either marketing copy or a list of notes.
> Neither tells you what a scent is actually like.
>
> So I built a map. 52 perfumes, placed by what their notes share.
> No photographs anywhere — everything is drawn from data.
>
> osmos.me

**2/6** — görsel: uzay karesi

> Position isn't decorative. Each perfume is read through three channels —
> scent family, character, shared notes — turned into a cosine distance and
> reduced to two dimensions with classical MDS.
>
> Things that sit close actually smell related.

**3/6** — görsel/video: dönen evrim imzası

> Every perfume has a signature that turns on its own: eight hours of
> opening, heart and base.
>
> Each note carries two numbers — peak minute and half-life. The curve is
> rise × decay. No charting library; it's all canvas.

**4/6** — görsel: nota sayfası

> 136 notes, each with its own page: how fast it rises, how long it lasts,
> and where it sits on four axes — cool/warm, velvet/sharp, dirty/clean,
> air/skin.
>
> The same four axes work as a search: "something cool and clean."

**5/6** — dürüstlük gönderisi (bunu atlama; en çok güven kazandıran gönderi)

> The honest part: the data is hand-written, not scraped. The volatility
> values, the character axes and the selection are mine — subjective by
> design.
>
> And the curve is a model, not a measurement. Real development shifts with
> skin and temperature.

**6/6** — kapanış + davet

> Free, no signup, nothing to buy. Works on phones.
> English and Turkish.
>
> If you know perfume: tell me where the map gets the neighbours wrong.
> That's the feedback I want.
>
> osmos.me
